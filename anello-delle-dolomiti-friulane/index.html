Anello delle Dolomiti Friulane

A rugged 4-day loop around the wild dolomites of the Friuli-Venezia-Giulia region.

Difficultyhard
Distance38 km
ParkingAt the parking lot about 100m below the Rifugio Pordenone

How to get there

From Pordenone, drive to Cimolais and take the forest road up to Rifugio Pordenone.
